Two Telecom Giants, One Choice to Make

The United Arab Emirates has two major telecom providers: Etisalat (now branded as e&) and du (from Emirates Integrated Telecommunications). Whether you are a long-time UAE resident, a new arrival, or switching from prepaid to postpaid, the choice between these two providers will affect your daily connectivity, call quality, and monthly bill for the length of your contract.

Both operators offer postpaid plans ranging from budget-friendly to ultra-premium. However, there are meaningful differences in network coverage, promotional pricing, international calling options, and unique features like VIP numbers. This guide breaks down every factor based on current promotions as of February 2026 so you can make an informed decision.

Network Coverage Comparison

Etisalat was the first telecom operator in the UAE and the first to launch 5G services in the country. This head start has resulted in a measurably wider network footprint, particularly in areas outside the major urban centres.

Coverage Area Etisalat (e&) du
5G Urban (Dubai, Abu Dhabi) Excellent Excellent
5G Suburban & Northern Emirates Strong Limited
Rural & Highway Coverage Reliable Patchy in remote areas
Building Penetration (Indoor) Strong Good
4G LTE Nationwide Near 100% Near 100%

Key takeaway: If you live in Dubai or Abu Dhabi and rarely travel outside city limits, both providers deliver comparable 5G speeds. However, if you commute between emirates, travel to Fujairah, Ras Al Khaimah, or Al Ain, or spend time in more rural areas, Etisalat provides noticeably more consistent connectivity. This is the result of its larger infrastructure investment and longer presence in the market.

International Calling Comparison

For the UAE's large expatriate population, international calling is often the deciding factor when choosing a telecom provider. This is an area where Etisalat has a distinct advantage.

Unlimited 1 Country Plan Available — unlimited calls to one country No equivalent
Flexi Minutes (Local + Intl) Available on Unlimited Calls Plan 600 Flexi International minutes sold as add-ons
International Add-on Packs Multiple options Multiple options
Countries Covered 200+ destinations 150+ destinations

Etisalat's Unlimited 1 Country Plan is a unique product in the UAE market. If you call India, Pakistan, Philippines, Bangladesh, Egypt, or any other single country frequently, you can get unlimited calling to that one destination. du does not offer a comparable product — international calls on du require purchasing add-on packs with fixed minute allotments.

Additionally, Etisalat's Unlimited Calls Plan 600 Flexi (currently at AED 360/month) includes flexi minutes that work for both local and international calls, giving users built-in international calling without needing a separate add-on.
