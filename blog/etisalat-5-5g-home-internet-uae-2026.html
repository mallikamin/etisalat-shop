What Is 5.5G Wireless Home Internet?

5.5G (also called 5G-Advanced) is the newest generation of the mobile network, a step beyond standard 5G in speed and responsiveness. For home internet, the idea is simple: instead of pulling fiber into your home, a wireless router connects to the 5.5G network and broadcasts normal home WiFi to all your devices.

What disappears in the process: the installation appointment, the cabling, the technician, the landline, and the upfront payment.
