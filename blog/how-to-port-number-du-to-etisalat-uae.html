How to Port Your Number from du to Etisalat in UAE 2026

Quick Answer

You can port your du number to Etisalat in 2 to 4 business days using Mobile Number Portability (MNP). You need your Emirates ID, your du number, and no outstanding contractual obligations with du. The process is free (TDRA regulation). We handle the entire porting process for you — just message us on WhatsApp with your du number and we will take care of everything.

Switching from du to Etisalat does not mean losing your phone number. Thanks to Mobile Number Portability (MNP), regulated by the UAE's Telecommunications and Digital Government Regulatory Authority (TDRA), you can keep your existing du number and transfer it to Etisalat's network. Your contacts, WhatsApp account, banking OTPs, and every service linked to your number continue working seamlessly.

This guide walks you through the complete porting process step by step, including what documents you need, how long it takes, what it costs, and how to avoid common problems. Whether you are switching for better coverage, a specific plan, or a VIP number, the process is the same.

What Is Mobile Number Portability (MNP)?

Mobile Number Portability is a TDRA-mandated service that allows UAE mobile subscribers to switch between operators (du and Etisalat) while keeping their existing phone number. It was introduced to give consumers the freedom to choose their preferred operator without the hassle of changing their number everywhere.

Key facts about MNP in the UAE:

  • Free to port — There is no charge for the MNP process itself. TDRA regulation prevents operators from charging for number porting.
  • 2 to 4 business days — The standard processing time from request submission to number activation on the new network.
  • No service interruption — Your du service continues working until the moment the port completes. There is typically only a brief 15-30 minute downtime during the actual switch.
  • Both prepaid and postpaid — MNP works for both prepaid and postpaid numbers.

Documents You Need to Port

Before starting the porting process, make sure you have the following ready:

  • Valid Emirates ID (front and back)
  • Your du mobile number that you want to port
  • Your du account must be active (not suspended or disconnected)
  • No outstanding du contract with early termination fees (or willingness to pay them)
  • All outstanding du bills must be settled
  • Your du number must have been active for at least 90 days
Before You Start

Check your du account status by dialing *135# from your du number or calling du customer care at 155. Confirm there are no unpaid bills, active contracts, or suspension issues. Resolving these before submitting the MNP request prevents delays.

Common Problems and Solutions

MNP Request Rejected

The most common reason for rejection is an outstanding du contract or unpaid bills. Contact du at 155 to settle any balances. If you have an active contract, ask about early termination fees. Once cleared, resubmit the MNP request.

Number Not Active for 90 Days

TDRA requires your number to have been active for at least 90 days before porting. If your du number is newer than 90 days, you will need to wait until this period passes before initiating the port.

Port Takes Longer Than Expected

If your port has not completed within 5 business days, contact us on WhatsApp and we will escalate with Etisalat. Delays can occur during public holidays or if there is a verification issue that needs manual resolution.

Lost SMS/Calls During Switch

There is typically a 15-30 minute window during the actual number transfer when you may not receive calls or SMS. This is normal. Once you insert the new Etisalat SIM and restart your phone, everything resumes. SMS sent during the brief switch window are typically delivered once the port completes.

WhatsApp and Banking Apps

WhatsApp, banking apps, and other services tied to your phone number continue to work after porting because the number itself does not change — only the network operator changes. You do not need to re-verify WhatsApp or update your number with your bank.
