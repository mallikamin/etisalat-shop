Frequently Asked Questions

What is Etisalat Business Pro Fiber?

Etisalat Business Pro Fiber is a dedicated high-speed office internet connection for UAE businesses, delivered over Etisalat (e&) business fibre. Each plan bundles a fixed business internet line with voice lines, Cloud PABX or a free 1.ae domain, a managed firewall, and a free device. It is built for offices, retail, clinics, and branches that need reliable, business-grade connectivity.

How much does Etisalat Business Pro Fiber cost?

There are six monthly plans, priced by speed: Business Pro 200 at AED 1,095, Business Pro 300 at AED 1,245, Business Pro 400 at AED 1,475, Business Pro 550 at AED 1,875, Business Pro 750 at AED 2,475, and Business Pro 1 Gbps at AED 3,375. All prices are monthly rental. Confirm final billing, including VAT, with our Etisalat business specialist.

What internet speeds does Business Pro Fiber offer?

Download speeds range from 200 Mbps on the entry plan up to 1 Gbps (1000 Mbps) on the top plan, with upload speeds from 20 Mbps up to 100 Mbps. Choose the speed tier that matches your office size and workload.

What is included with each Business Pro Fiber plan?

Every plan includes business voice lines (from 1 line on Business Pro 200 up to 10 lines on Business Pro 1 Gbps), a managed firewall, and a free device. The lower plans include Cloud PABX, while the higher plans include a free 1.ae domain. Confirm Cloud PABX and the included features for your chosen plan with our specialist.
