How Does Golden Number Pricing Work in the UAE?

The single most common misconception about UAE golden numbers is that there is a one-time "purchase price" for the number itself — like buying a product outright. That is not how Etisalat structures it. Golden numbers, VIP numbers, and fancy numbers are all part of the same system: the number is bundled with an Etisalat postpaid plan, and the plan's monthly fee is what you pay.

Etisalat (now branded as e&) classifies premium numbers into three tiers — Silver, Gold, and Platinum — each tied to a corresponding plan level. When you commit to a plan for 24 months, you get the number for the life of that plan, and then it is permanently linked to your account. There is no SIM fee on top of that, and no separate number reservation charge.

This means the real question is not "how much does the number cost?" but rather "which plan tier does my chosen number require, and is that monthly commitment the right fit for my budget?" The answer to that determines your total cost of ownership.

What Factors Determine Which Tier a Number Falls Into?

Not every visually interesting number qualifies as Gold or Platinum. Etisalat classifies numbers by the mathematical rarity and memorability of their digit patterns. Understanding this helps you know what to expect when browsing inventory.

  • Pattern rarity: The fewer times a given digit sequence appears across all possible 7-digit suffixes, the rarer — and higher-tier — the number. Quadruple repeated digits (0000, 1111) are extremely rare by pure probability. Double repeated pairs (5500, 7700) are more common and land in Silver.
  • Sequential strength: Full ascending or descending runs (1234567, 9876543) are classed Gold or Platinum depending on how clean and complete the run is. Partial runs (only the last 3–4 digits) are typically Silver.
  • Mirror and palindrome patterns: Numbers where the suffix reads the same forwards and backwards (e.g., 12321) are classified Gold. Longer or more perfect palindromes reach Platinum.
  • Culturally significant digits: In the UAE market, numbers with multiple 7s, 8s, or 0s carry higher perceived value and are classified accordingly. A number ending in 0007 is Gold; ending in 0000 is Platinum.
  • Number prefix: The 050 prefix is the oldest and most recognized Etisalat prefix. Numbers on 050 with strong patterns are often classified at a higher tier than the same pattern on 056 or 055, reflecting demand.

The upgrade cost is not always as large as the headline price suggests.

No. Etisalat golden and VIP numbers are not sold as standalone products. The number is bundled with an Etisalat postpaid plan subscription. The monthly plan fee is the only cost — there is no additional SIM fee, number reservation fee, or upfront purchase charge on top of the plan.

Yes. All Etisalat postpaid plans are subject to UAE VAT at 5%. The stated monthly prices (AED 188, AED 500, AED 1,000) are before VAT. Your billed amount each month will be the monthly rate multiplied by 1.05 — for example, the Gold plan bills at AED 525/month including VAT.
