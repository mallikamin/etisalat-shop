A plug-and-play home internet service that runs on the Etisalat mobile network instead of a fixed landline. You receive a wireless router (5G router optional), plug it in, and your home WiFi is live — no technician visit, no landline installation. Ideal for villas, apartments, and areas where fiber installation is slow or unavailable.
