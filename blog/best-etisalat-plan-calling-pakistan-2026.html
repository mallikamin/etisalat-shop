Tips for Calling Pakistan from UAE

WhatsApp Calls vs Regular Calls

Many Pakistani expats rely on WhatsApp for international calls because it uses data instead of minutes. Here is a practical comparison:

  • WhatsApp voice calls: Free (uses data), but quality depends on internet connection on both ends. Works well on Wi-Fi, can be patchy on mobile data in congested areas. Not ideal for business calls or emergencies.
  • WhatsApp video calls: Use more data (roughly 5 MB per minute). Good for family catch-ups but can drop in quality if either party has a weak connection.
  • Etisalat regular calls: Clear, consistent voice quality using the telecom network. No dependency on internet speed. Works everywhere, including areas with no Wi-Fi. Calls connect instantly without needing the recipient to have the app open.
Practical Tip

The best approach is to use both. Use your Etisalat plan's flexi or unlimited minutes for important calls (parents, business, emergencies) where you need reliable audio quality. Use WhatsApp for casual, longer conversations where the occasional audio dip does not matter. With 27 GB or 50 GB of data in your plan, you have plenty of bandwidth for WhatsApp calls.

How the Preferred Country Feature Works

The Unlimited 1 Country Plan 325 includes a preferred country feature. When you subscribe, you choose one country (Pakistan, in this case) and all calls to that country become unlimited. Here is what to know:

  • You select Pakistan at the time of activation
  • Both Pakistani mobile and landline numbers are included
  • Your 700 flexi minutes remain available for local UAE calls and calls to other countries
  • Changing your preferred country may require contacting Etisalat or your sales representative

Dialing Pakistan from UAE

To call a Pakistani number from your Etisalat line, dial: 00 92 (or +92) followed by the number without the leading zero. For mobile numbers, drop the initial 0 from the 11-digit number (for example, 00 92 300 XXXXXXX for a Jazz mobile, 00 92 345 XXXXXXX for a Telenor number). For landlines, dial 00 92 followed by the city code and number (for example, 00 92 42 XXXXXXXX for Lahore, 00 92 21 XXXXXXXX for Karachi). All calls to Pakistan (mobile and landline) use the same flexi minutes or preferred country benefit.
