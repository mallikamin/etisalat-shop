What determines a golden number's price

Two golden numbers on the same plan can have very different prices. Pattern rarity is why:

  • Repeating digits (0500 000 000) are the rarest and most valuable, usually Platinum.
  • Clean sequences (0561 234 567) and short memorable endings (…7 8 6, …1 1 1) sit in Gold or Platinum.
  • Tidy, easy-to-recall patterns with a couple of repeats are Silver, and often free with a plan.
  • Prefix also matters, some customers prefer the classic 050 or 056 range.
