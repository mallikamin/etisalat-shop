Quick Answer

Etisalat offers two Home Wireless plans in 2026: Advance at AED 206/month (promotional price, standard AED 229; 12-month commitment) and Premium at AED 269/month (promotional price, standard AED 299; 24-month commitment). Both include unlimited local data, an optional 5G router, and free 24-hour delivery. Premium adds STARZPLAY streaming and free GoChat Premium. Prices exclude 5% VAT. Pick Advance for the shorter, cheaper commitment; pick Premium for bundled entertainment.

Home Wireless Advance in Detail

Advance is the entry plan, and for most households it is the rational default. You get the same unlimited local data as Premium, the same optional 5G router, and the same free 24-hour delivery — at AED 63/month less and with a commitment half as long.

The bundled extra is GoChat with unlimited voice & video calls — useful if your household makes a lot of app-based calls.

Choose Advance if:

  • You rent on a 12-month tenancy contract and do not want a commitment longer than your lease.
  • You want the lowest monthly cost for unlimited home data.
  • You already have streaming subscriptions and do not need STARZPLAY.

Home Wireless Premium in Detail

Premium costs AED 63/month more and doubles the commitment to 24 months. In exchange you get two bundled extras: a STARZPLAY streaming subscription and GoChat Premium free.

The value question is simple: if you would pay for STARZPLAY anyway, Premium effectively discounts your internet by the subscription's price every month. If you would not, the extras do not justify the longer lock-in by themselves.

Choose Premium if:

  • You (or your family) actively watch STARZPLAY content — the bundle does the math for you.
  • You own your home or expect to stay 2+ years at the same address.
  • You want one bill covering internet and entertainment.
