Postpaid vs Prepaid (8)

Are your Etisalat numbers prepaid or postpaid?

All our numbers and plans are Etisalat POSTPAID — monthly-billing plans from AED 188 to AED 1,000 per month with unlimited data. We do not sell prepaid SIMs.

What is the difference between prepaid and postpaid?

Prepaid means you top up credit before using the line. Postpaid means you get a monthly plan with a fixed bill — unlimited data, bundled minutes, and a free VIP number included. Everything we sell is postpaid.

Postpaid gives you unlimited data, a fixed predictable monthly bill, far better per-dirham value, and a free VIP golden number — none of which prepaid offers.

Will I get a bill every month on postpaid?

Yes — Etisalat bills you monthly. You can pay via the My Etisalat UAE app, online, or at any Etisalat channel.

If I order a VIP number from you, is it automatically postpaid?
