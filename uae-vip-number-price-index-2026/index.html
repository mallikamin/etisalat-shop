How VIP Number Pricing Works in the UAE

Unlike many countries where a vanity number carries a large standalone price tag, in the UAE the number is bundled with a postpaid plan. You pay the monthly plan; the matching VIP number is included free. That's why a "golden number price" question doesn't have a single sticker price — it's the plan tier that sets the cost.

Silver, Gold & Platinum — what separates the tiers

Silver numbers have memorable repeating or mirrored endings and are free with any plan from AED 188/month. Gold numbers carry stronger patterns (sequences like 1234, mirror pairs like 1221) and come with the Gold Plan at AED 500/month. Platinum numbers are the rarest — quadruple repeats (1111, 7777) and ascending runs (23456) — paired with the Platinum Plan at AED 1,000/month.

When is there a one-time number fee?

Only for rare, hand-picked standalone numbers that someone specifically wants outside the bundled tiers. Those range from around AED 500 to AED 50,000+ for the most exclusive patterns. For the vast majority of buyers, the number is simply free with the plan.

Is the price different in Dubai vs other emirates?

No — Etisalat postpaid pricing is uniform UAE-wide. A VIP number in Dubai costs the same as in Abu Dhabi or Sharjah. Delivery is free and same-day in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah, and 1–2 days elsewhere.
